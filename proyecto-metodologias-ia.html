How AI transformed the way we worked on the Tasks and Plans project

CLIENT UBITS
ROLE Product Designer
TEAM PM, Design, UX, Dev
DURATION 8 months

This was not just a UI design project

It was a complete redesign of how we work as a team. We integrated AI into the daily work of PM, design and research, transforming linear processes into collaborative and agile cycles.

THE PROBLEM

How did we work before?

01

Silos between areas

Each one in their stage. PM defined, design executed, UX validated at the end.

02

Linear processes

Many handoffs, little iteration. From idea to prototype: weeks.

03

Manual documentation

Hours of work on specs and flows. Time that was not used to think.

"We needed a methodology that would allow us to think, decide and execute faster as a team, using AI as the central muscle of the process."
THE SOLUTION

AI as the team's "third brain"

The deepest change was not using AI as a tool, but integrating it as another member of the team.

🧠

Expanded thinking

We explore more alternatives in less time. From "A or B" to "A, B, C, D, E and combinations".

⚡

Accelerated execution

From idea to functional prototype in hours. Real code, not just mockups.

🤝

Amplified collaboration

PM, design and dev on the same material. One version, built together.

THE METHODOLOGY

4 stages of the new flow

01

Discovery with AI

BEFORE

Long meetings to align. Extensive documents that no one read.

NOW

AI synthesizes context, generates key questions and creates alignment documents in minutes.

💡 AI use: We convert loose discussions into clear and prioritized user stories.
02

Real-time co-creation

BEFORE

Each area in its stage. Silos and strong dependencies.

NOW

Joint sessions where we build in real time. Decisions in the moment.

💡 AI use: AI works as "third brain" in sessions. We prototype ideas live.
03

Accelerated prototyping

BEFORE

From idea to prototype: weeks. Many internal iterations.

NOW

From idea to functional prototype: hours. Cursor for real code.

💡 AI use: Interactive prototypes with real code. We reduce distance between "idea" and "validation".
04

Intelligent documentation

BEFORE

Documenting took as long as designing. Manual and tedious work.

NOW

We automate documentation. We free up time to think about the problem.

💡 AI use: AI generates technical specs, edge cases and documentation while we iterate the design.
IMPACT

The numbers speak

-60% Planning time
-70% Documentation time
3x More iterations per sprint
2h Instead of 1 week to align
RESULTS

What did we achieve?

✓

Real collaboration

The team went from working in silos to co-creating in real time.

✓

Shorter cycles

More iterations, better final quality, less wasted time.

✓

Better product

Tasks and Plans launched with speed and validated quality.

✓

Replicable methodology

It is now the standard for new projects at UBITS.

Habiitemos

App designed to simplify the search for shared housing, connecting users with compatible roommates through clear profiles, intelligent filters and an intuitive experience. The project transforms an informal and disorganized process into a safe, guided and fast solution for young people, students and professionals who need to find a space and a suitable life partner.

Purpose to solve

Users need a reliable, fast and clear way to find shared housing and compatible roommates, avoiding informal searches and disorganized processes.

Insights
Young people, students or professionals in housing transition.
They value security, clarity in profiles and compatibility.
They use mobile apps as the main search tool.
They feel frustrated by incomplete or unreliable information.

The user seeks a space where they can filter options, understand if a roommate is compatible and contact easily, all from a clear and frictionless experience.

Wants to see real compatibility in lifestyle, schedules and habits.

Needs to compare options quickly.

Prefers short processes and functions that do not require a learning curve.

Seeks to make safe decisions without depending on informal groups or social networks.

Design Process

01

Context

I explored how people search for shared housing, identifying common frustrations, real needs and relevant compatibility criteria. This allowed defining a clear user: young people and professionals seeking a safer and more reliable process.

02

Problem Definition

From the findings, the main challenge was framed: roommate search is disorganized, manual and unreliable. Pain points, user objectives and key opportunities for a digital solution were structured.

03

Product Architecture & Flow

User flows and functional structure were created: guided onboarding, profile exploration, intelligent filters, compatibility matching and direct contact. The logic focused on reducing friction and showing the essential first.

04

UI/UX Design

A clean, accessible and clear interface was developed, focused on transparency and ease of use. Screens highlighted key information about profiles, habits and housing to support quick and safe decisions.

05

Validation & Adjustments

Flows and screens were tested with users to verify clarity, ease and confidence. Findings allowed adjusting content, simplifying steps and reinforcing the feeling of security during the search.

Value Proposition

A clear, safe and compatibility-focused experience to find shared housing. The app transforms an informal and chaotic process into an intuitive solution with verified profiles, intelligent filters and a guided flow that facilitates quick and reliable decisions.

Key points
01 Verified profiles with relevant and transparent information.
02 Intelligent filters based on lifestyle and habits.
03 Matching system that prioritizes real compatibility.
04 Intuitive flow that reduces friction and speeds up the search.
05 Experience centered on security and informed decisions.
